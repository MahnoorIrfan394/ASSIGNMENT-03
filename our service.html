STOMATA

These are microscopic opening present in the epidermis of leaves. Through these openings, plants exchange gases with their environment.Each stoma is a slit like opening formed by two special cells called guard cells.They are chlorophyll containing cells with thicker inner while thinner and elastic outer cell walls.The opening and closing of stomata depends upon turgidity of the guard cells.

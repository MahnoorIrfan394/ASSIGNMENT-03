GASEOUS EXCHANGE IN ANIMALS

Like plants animals do exchange gases with their environment for the respiration process. In order to obtain energy from food, they taken in oxygen and give out carbondioxide .So the process of gaseous exchange is ultimately linked with the respiration.The respiration medium for aduatic animals is water whereas for terrestrial animals is air.The amount of molecular oxygen present in air is about 21% while in water it is about 5%.
